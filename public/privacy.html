CityNovus

Privacy policy

Effective 6 September 2026. Applies to citynovus.com and the CityNovus web app, operated from Guwahati, Assam, India. Contact: parthajy@gmail.com.

In short

CityNovus is a free, community-built map. We keep the minimum needed to run a shared map with accounts: a display name, an email address if you sign in, and the things you build. Your email is never shown publicly and never sold. You can delete your account yourself from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Account data if you sign in: your email address, your name, and your profile picture as provided by Google (see below), plus an internal account ID.
  • Display name you choose. It is shown publicly on everything you build.
  • Contributions: buildings, roads, farms, trees, landmarks and other plots you add or edit, names you give them, photos you upload, confirmations, flags, wishlist votes, and the points and coins you earn.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and basic product events (for example that you opened the app, finished onboarding, or published something), kept for security, abuse prevention and understanding which features are used.
  • Location: only when you tap the locate button, only in your browser, to move the map. It is not sent to us or stored.

Google sign-in

When you choose "Continue with Google", we request the OpenID Connect scopes openid, email and profile. From Google we receive your email address, your name, your profile picture URL and a Google account identifier. We use them only to create and sign you into your CityNovus account, to show your name and picture in the app, and to carry over any work you did as a guest. We do not read your contacts, calendar, Drive, Gmail or anything else, and we do not request access to them.

CityNovus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties except as needed to operate the service (our hosting provider) or as required by law. You can revoke CityNovus's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How we use data

  • To run the map: show your contributions to everyone, credit them to your display name, and keep your points and coins.
  • To keep you signed in (one session cookie, valid up to 90 days, renewed while you keep visiting).
  • To prevent abuse: rate limits, sign-up caps and moderation use your IP address and account history.
  • To improve the product using aggregate usage statistics.

We do not make automated decisions with legal effect about you, and we do not profile you for advertising.

What is public

Your display name, the plots you own or built, their names and photos, and your position on leaderboards. Your email address, IP address and Google account identifier are never public.

Contributions are open data

Map contributions are published under the Open Database License 1.0 so the map can never be taken private and can be contributed back to OpenStreetMap. Contributions are credited to your display name only.

Sharing and third parties

  • Hosting: DigitalOcean (servers and database) stores our data.
  • Google: sign-in as described above, and Google Analytics, which counts visits and feature use, sets its own cookies and sends usage data to Google. See how Google uses this data.
  • Map and weather services: your browser requests basemap tiles from OpenFreeMap, optional satellite imagery from Esri, weather from Open-Meteo and fonts from Google Fonts. They see your IP address, as any website resource does.

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with these providers to run the service, or where the law requires.

Cookies and browser storage

One session cookie to keep you signed in, Google Analytics cookies, and browser storage for preferences such as satellite mode and whether you have seen the intro.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Guest work that is never saved to an account is deleted automatically after 24 hours. Technical logs and product events are kept for up to 90 days. Contributions remain on the map, as open data, after account deletion.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can see everything tied to your account in the app. To delete your account, open the menu in the app and choose "Delete my account", or email parthajy@gmail.com from the account's address. Deletion removes your email address, Google identifier, profile picture and display name; sessions are ended immediately. Your contributions stay on the map credited to "a former player", because they are open data that others have built on. You may also ask us to correct or export your data at the same address.

Children

CityNovus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords, where used, are stored only as salted hashes. Access to the database is limited to the operator.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note the new date here and, for signed-in users, say so in the app.
